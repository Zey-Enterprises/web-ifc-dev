* comprehensive application and intake review  
* 90-minute onboarding session  
* weekly progress review and feedback as frequently as once per week, including substantive evaluation, recommendations, and appropriate plan adjustments related to Client's coaching work  
* ad hoc written support for clarification and troubleshooting related to Client's coaching work  
* referral or tandem-support discussion when a need belongs with a licensed professional  
* 3-month initial engagement, billed monthly

If either party exercises this early off-ramp, Services will end at the end of the first billing period, no further monthly payments will be due under the initial 3-month engagement or applicable custom package term, and the first payment is not refundable. Any unpaid amounts for additional Services remain due. The early off-ramp is not a satisfaction guarantee and does not create a right to a refund.

## Onboarding Session

The onboarding session is the first substantive coaching session under this Addendum. Its purpose is to establish the initial working context, clarify goals and constraints, review relevant intake information, identify priorities, and set the initial direction for coaching.

The onboarding session may include, as appropriate, the following:

* review of Client's application, intake form, goals, history, and current circumstances  
* clarification of the primary and secondary areas of focus  
* initial prioritization of coaching work  
* identification of relevant constraints, risks, and required outside professional support  
* establishment of initial coaching expectations and communication norms  
* initial direction for physical exercise, nutrition, emotional regulation, intellectual development, habits, recovery, or other relevant areas of Fitness Development

The onboarding session does not guarantee a complete training program, nutrition plan, habit plan, written analysis, or other deliverable by the end of that session. Any specific deliverables are subject to the package scope, coaching priorities, timing, and Coach's professional judgment.

If Client cancels, reschedules, or fails to attend the onboarding session, the [Scheduling and Cancellations](/coaching-agreement/#scheduling-and-cancellations) section of [the Agreement](/coaching-agreement/) applies.

## Weekly Progress Review and Feedback

Weekly progress review and feedback is a structured coaching process in which Client provides relevant updates, data, questions, and context, and Coach reviews that information and provides substantive evaluation, recommendations, and appropriate plan adjustments related to Client's coaching work.

Weekly progress review and feedback may be used to review progress, identify obstacles, assess adherence, evaluate implementation, clarify relevant context, and determine whether adjustments are appropriate.

Client is responsible for providing the information needed for meaningful review. The relevant information may include bodyweight, nutrition data, physical exercise logs, habit or practice completion, recovery, sleep, stress, obstacles, questions, reflections, or other information relevant to Client's coaching work. Information does not need to be resubmitted if it is already available to Coach through an agreed shared tool, spreadsheet, app, or log.

Coach will provide feedback within 72 hours of Client's complete submission, normally sooner when feasible. Feedback is available as frequently as once per week. If Client submits late, inconsistently, or less often than weekly, Coach will respond after submission, but Client may receive fewer review-and-feedback cycles during that month.

Weekly progress review and feedback is not unlimited back-and-forth. Coach may ask clarifying questions when needed. ## Renewal, Cancellation, and Notice

If this Addendum provides for monthly Services, Client acknowledges that monthly Services renew month-to-month after the initial 3-month engagement or applicable custom package term, unless discontinued by either party with at least 14 days' written notice before the next renewal date.

Either party may discontinue monthly Services by providing written notice as required by this Addendum and [the Agreement](/coaching-agreement/). Client may provide written notice to Coach by email at: arthur@integratedfitnesscoaching.com

If Client entered into this Addendum through an online medium, Coach may provide an online cancellation mechanism. If such a mechanism is provided, Client may use that mechanism to discontinue monthly Services. Use of an online cancellation mechanism provided by Coach is considered valid written cancellation.

Cancellation by any other mechanism is not considered valid unless confirmed in writing via email by Coach.

Cancellation is effective only for future billing periods and does not entitle Client to a refund of fees already paid, except as expressly stated in [the Agreement](/coaching-agreement/), this Addendum, or as required by law.

If any material renewal, fee, cadence, or cancellation term changes, Coach will provide notice to Client in a manner that can be retained by Client. Continued Services may require a signed updated Addendum or other written confirmation.

## Scope Boundaries and Outside Professional Support

Client acknowledges that the Services are educational and advisory and do not constitute medical care, psychotherapy, psychiatry, physical therapy, rehabilitation, medical nutrition therapy, diagnosis or treatment of eating disorders, legal advice, or emergency/crisis support.

Coach may recommend that Client seek support from a licensed professional, including a physician, psychotherapist, psychiatrist, physical therapist, registered dietitian, or other qualified professional.

Coach may decline, suspend, modify, or terminate Services if, in Coach's reasonable judgment, Client's needs exceed the scope of coaching, require licensed professional care, or cannot be appropriately integrated with outside professional support.

Coaching may proceed alongside licensed professional support when appropriate, but coaching does not replace such support.

## Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to the following:

* provide accurate, complete, and timely information in application, intake, health disclosure, onboarding, and coaching communications  
* promptly update Coach about relevant changes in physical health, injuries, medications, limitations, mental health, emotional state, psychological condition, schedule, life circumstances, goals, constraints, or other factors relevant to coaching  
* provide relevant updates, data, questions, and context needed for weekly progress review and feedback  
* exercise independent judgment in deciding whether and how to implement coaching recommendations  
* monitor Client's own physical and mental condition  
* seek appropriate professional care when needed  
* participate in coaching in good faith  
* pay all fees when due  
* respect scheduling, cancellation, and communication boundaries

Client understands that the effectiveness of coaching depends substantially on Client's effort, consistency, communication, and judgment.
