A multivitamin can be a reasonable backup when intake is limited, calories are low, food variety is poor, or a clinician recommends one. It is not a replacement for a nutritious diet.

Evidence does not support treating multivitamins as a general solution for cardiovascular disease or cancer prevention in otherwise well-nourished adults.{% include citation.html id="alexander-2013-a-systematic-review-of-multivitamin-multimineral-use-and-cardiovascular-disease-and-cancer-incidence-and-total-mortality" label="1" %}

If you suspect a deficiency, testing and targeted replacement are often better than guessing with high doses.
