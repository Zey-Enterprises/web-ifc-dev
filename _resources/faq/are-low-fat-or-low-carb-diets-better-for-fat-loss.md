Neither is automatically better. Fat loss depends primarily on {% include glossary.html id="calorie-balance" text="calorie balance" %}. Low-fat and low-carb diets can both work when they create a sustainable calorie deficit.

Choose based on adherence, training, hunger, food preferences, health markers, and social life. Low-carb may reduce food options and training carbs for some people. Low-fat may be less satisfying for others.

The winning diet is the one that creates the needed deficit while preserving protein, fiber, {% include glossary.html id="micronutrients" %}, training performance, and mental wellbeing.
