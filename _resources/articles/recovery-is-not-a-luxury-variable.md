Recovery determines whether productive work can continue. Sleep, stress load, fatigue, and emotional strain affect appetite, training performance, decision quality, and consistency. When recovery is poor, people often misdiagnose the problem as laziness or lack of discipline. A recoverable plan is a plan that accounts for human limits while still demanding progress. That is not a lower standard. It is what makes higher standards executable.
