Bodyweight is not a moral score, and it is not the only factor that matters for health. It still mediates many other concerns: sleep quality, cardiometabolic risk, joint stress, movement tolerance, training capacity, energy, and sometimes self-efficacy. A reality-based view of bodyweight does not require shame or aesthetic conformity. It requires taking the body seriously as the biological platform through which health, action, and flourishing are pursued.
