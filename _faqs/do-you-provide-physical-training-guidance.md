Yes.

Physical training guidance can include programming direction, exercise selection, progression strategy, workload calibration, recovery management, and review of whether your plan fits your goals, equipment, schedule, and current capacity.

Depending on the coaching package and scope, this may be as light as helping you adjust an existing program or as involved as developing a more specific structure for your physical training.

The purpose is not complexity for its own sake. The purpose is to help your physical exercise serve your actual goals while remaining sustainable enough to perform consistently.
