Yes.

Nutrition coaching is a core part of the practice. That may include calorie strategy, protein targets, meal structure, food choices, adherence planning, diet phases, maintenance after weight loss, and practical decision-making around work, travel, restaurants, family life, and social events.

The goal is not to hand you a brittle diet that only works under perfect conditions. The goal is to help you understand what matters most, execute it realistically, and adjust intelligently when life changes.

Nutrition coaching is not medical nutrition therapy. I do not diagnose or treat medical conditions, prescribe therapeutic diets for disease, or replace care from a physician, registered dietitian, or other qualified medical professional.
