concerns:
tags:
---

Yes, when it fits the coaching goal and package scope.

Nutrition work may include specific calorie targets, protein targets, macro targets, meal structure, food-choice guidance, adherence strategies, and diet-phase planning.

Physical training work may include exercise selection, weekly structure, progression guidance, workload adjustments, and programming direction.

But not every client needs the same level of prescription. Sometimes the highest-leverage move is a specific plan. Sometimes it is a simpler structure, a better routine, a better food environment, or a clearer understanding of what to prioritize.

The amount of detail depends on your goals, current knowledge, adherence, constraints, and coaching package.
